The five data families

A compact model that remains extensible for different laboratories, NOMAD and future AI.

1
Ownership and scope

User and Workspace identify ownership. Project is an optional grouping and never replaces Process.

UserAccount · Workspace · Project
2
Workflow definitions

Versioned Processes and workflow steps define what an experiment expects without storing actual results.

ProcessDefinition · WorkflowStepDefinition
3
Reusable resources

Definitions and physical inventory are distinct and searchable.

Material · MaterialLot · SolutionRecipe · Protocol · Instrument
4
Experimental execution

Snapshots record actual preparation, stack variants, samples, actions, parameters and deviations.

Experiment · ExperimentStep · SolutionBatch · ExperimentStack · ProcessRun · Sample
5
Evidence and outputs

Files retain type and provenance; results remain linked to source evidence and validation state.

FileRecord · Measurement · DerivedResult · Report · ExportPackage
AI and NOMAD remain optional consumersThe core workflow works without either service.

Raw data, processed data, human notes and AI outputs remain explicitly distinguishable. Stable identifiers, units and provenance make later mapping possible without redesigning the user model.

# JV measurements

Observed JV files may contain general metadata, measurement settings, cell settings, FW/RV metric rows and raw curve points.

Core metrics include Voc, Jsc, Vmpp, Jmpp, Pmpp, Rs, Rsh, FF and efficiency/PCE. LabFlow preserves the measurement/source relationship and derives aggregate comparison metrics such as hysteresis deterministically from matched scans where defined.

Raw curves and authoritative numerical metrics are never generated by AI. AI interpretation consumes deterministic results as evidence.
